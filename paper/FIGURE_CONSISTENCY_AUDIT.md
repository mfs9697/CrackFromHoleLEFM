## Formatting and integration

All 30 vector panels were rendered and inspected. The reference/COD/quality groups were redrawn only in a scratch directory for comparison; their 15 working-tree PDFs were pixel-identical at the audit rendering resolution. Several older Git versions had clipped axis labels or differed from the current generating routine. The already-correct, source-matching working-tree vectors were included in the data/figure commit without changing their starting bytes. Five previously untracked included vectors (three mesh illustrations and two unaffected increment panels) were also packaged unchanged so a clean checkout can compile.

Times New Roman plotting typography, mathematical notation, restrained blue/orange/green/purple roles, marker styles and vector output were retained. No scientifically correct panel was redesigned for cosmetic uniformity. The increment direction label/export correction and interpolation-caption clarification address meaning rather than appearance.

The final manuscript was compiled with Tectonic and rendered for visual inspection of all 24 pages, including every figure/subfigure and both corrected tables. No clipped plot labels, overlapping annotations, illegible legends or broken graphic references were found. Narrow subcaption underfull-box advisories remain; they do not produce clipping or missing text. Two existing sentences and the new control-description sentence were rephrased only to avoid overfull text lines. Figure numbering remains 1-8 and S1; every LaTeX label and figure inclusion order is unchanged.

## Reproducibility and scope

- Authoritative isolated publication records: 8 fixed reference geometries and 23 independently propagated states, all physically accepted; both completed archives agree in checked mechanical records.
- Full-precision JSON is the plotting authority. CSVs are inspection/export copies. The manifest hashes both and pins the unchanged reference snapshot and source archive files.
- `load_isolated_tip_publication_data` requires isolated identity, core scale 2 / exterior scale 1, complete P1-P23 history, correct fixed states, accepted records and matching bytes. Missing/corrupt data stop plotting. Historical coupled MAT/CSV fallback is removed.
- A sandbox with only portable inputs and rendering code reproduced the corrected figure. Nine publication-input regressions reject missing, historical, rehashed wrong-family, incomplete and unaccepted records.
- Independent extraction/arithmetic comparisons checked 153 replacement quantities against the earlier read-only archive assessment. `verify_isolated_tip_correction.py` checks 59 portable data/table quantities, 31 accepted records, all nine groups/30 assets, captions' scientific scope and absence of historical fallback.
- Baseline hashes confirm that all original numerical archives, accepted reference data, historical coupled data, solver/formulation/gate sources and unaffected plotted assets retain their starting bytes. Changes to graph content are limited to the four corrected tip panels and the increment direction label and trajectory legend/export sizing. Packaging pre-existing vectors changes Git inclusion, not their values or appearance.

**Unresolved limitations:** the original M1 history and the mesh-hierarchy coordinates/connectivity are unavailable as saved inputs. Their existing figures were retained and their limits are explicit above. No new physical or mesh calculation was substituted to conceal either limitation.
