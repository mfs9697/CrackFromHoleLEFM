## Construction and preservation

One constrained upper triangulation uses concentric rings with smooth target
spacing

`h(r) = 0.056 mm + 0.079 mm * (1-exp(-(max(0,r-0.5 mm)/1.3 mm)^2))`.

The first ring is fixed at the stored tip target. Subsequent radial spacing
is `0.86*h`; angular counts follow `ceil(pi*r/h)`. The entire upper topology
is reflected to form the lower topology. Negative-axis nodes behind the tip
are duplicated by topology; the tip and positive-axis intact ligament are
shared. T6 midsides are generated by the unchanged `T3toT6_fast` and inherit
that rule. No quarter-point elements are introduced.

A separate constrained Delaunay collar joins the paired polygon to the exact
old cavity edges. Its interfaces and crack line remain fixed during eight
bounded smoothing steps, with Delaunay connectivity restored every two
steps. A dimensionless area filter rejects zero-interior hull artifacts
caused by roundoff at collinear original boundary subdivisions. Exact
interface incidence, boundary equality and area coverage are subsequently
required; the filter does not permit deletion of a real cavity cell.

All **19,630 original triangles outside the cavity are retained exactly**, up
to the saved node renumbering map. Their used coordinates are bitwise equal
to the originals. Canonical noncrack physical boundary segments are bitwise
identical before and after remeshing; the crack endpoints/centerline and
stored material are identical. The full asymmetric exterior therefore stays
in place, including the retained hole, mouth and physical boundary nodes.
Existing loading, anchors and BC definitions are not changed or re-evaluated.
The candidate is a mesh artifact to be combined with the existing physical
problem in a separately authorized experiment.

The exact replaced triangle indices are
`candidate.replacedOriginalElementIDs` (19,811 entries), with the complementary
`retainedOriginalElementIDs` and `originalNodeToCandidate` map. No approximate
geometric reconstruction is needed to replay this candidate.

Shape is `4*sqrt(3)*area/sum(edgeLength.^2)`, with 1 equilateral.
The paired patch contains 16,146 triangles with minimum angle 25.9941 deg;
the collar contains 3,211 with minimum angle 24.0518 deg. The maximum ratio
of longest edges across adjacent affected/interface elements is 1.728694,
below the prospective 2.5 grading criterion. The minimum 16-point T6
Jacobian determinant is `1.3009135909e-9 m^2`, and every midpoint-coordinate
check passes.

All 13 candidate tip-incident topological edges are 0.0540246507854 mm.
Step38 has seven incident edges spanning 0.0521583--0.0616974 mm, median
0.0534277 mm. The candidate matches the stored target essentially exactly,
and lies inside the original incident-edge range. The prospective target
tolerance is 5%.

The important inner-annulus 0.8--1.2 mm shell has longest-edge median
0.075972 mm versus 0.070493 mm, and p90 0.091231 versus 0.082706 mm.
At 4.8--5.2 mm, the median is approximately 0.154 versus 0.147 mm and the
p90 approximately 0.173 versus 0.170 mm. Resolution is comparable, but not
identical shell by shell. In the historically refined 3.2--4.0 mm shell the
candidate median is about 0.153 versus 0.120 mm; its p90 is slightly smaller.
All shell counts, medians, p90 values and minimum angles are exported in
`_radialTable.csv`, including unchanged shells outside the cavity.

This is a more regular topology with a much improved worst shape/angle and
smoother grading. It is **not a substantial element-count reduction**:
total triangles fall 1.15%, affected triangles 2.29%, and primary support
elements 7.11%. Comparable resolution was prioritized over an arbitrary
target count. Median shape is slightly lower, despite improved worst shape.

## Prescribed-field qualification

After structural acceptance, the unchanged production
`SIF_LEFM_interaction_EDI` is called with `QuadratureRule=16`,
`WeightFunction='fe_nodal'`, and only the exact 0.8--5.2 mm annulus.
Williams fields are prescribed at nodes using topological upper/lower
face-side angles, rotated into the global frame. No equilibrium solution
is involved. The independent tiny input is `(KI,KII)=(1,1e-4)`; neither
input is taken from the physical Step38 SIF result.

| Prescribed (KI, KII) | Recovered KI | Recovered KII |
|---|---:|---:|
| (1, 0) | 1.00000000063490 | +6.6035114807e-15 |
| (0, 1) | -4.6353952566e-15 | 0.999999980210828 |
| (1, 1e-4) | 1.00000000063489 | 9.99999980273001e-5 |

Every production call uses 14,632 elements under its literal participation
logic. Pure-I leakage is at floating-point scale; pure-II sign and unit
normalization are preserved, so Mode II has not been suppressed. Tiny mixed
relative KII error is `1.9727e-8`. The affine interpolation/gradient control
and linear-superposition control also pass.
The maximum affine error is `1.6371e-10`, the recovery-matrix Frobenius error
is `1.9799e-8`, and the superposition error is `8.6683e-15`.

Prospective synthetic criteria: affine error <=1e-8; pure-I cross-mode ratio
and pure-II cross-mode magnitude <=1e-10; recovery-matrix Frobenius error
<=2e-4; tiny mixed relative KII error <=2e-4; superposition error <=1e-10.
These qualification thresholds do not estimate physical KII uncertainty.
